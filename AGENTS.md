# Agent instructions for this fork

This checkout is the Abnormal-owned fork at [abnormalhq/stalwart](https://github.com/abnormalhq/stalwart), based on upstream Stalwart v1.1.0. Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for fork-specific routing.

## Do not contact upstream

Do not open or draft GitHub issues or pull requests against `stalwartlabs/stalwart`, contact Stalwart Labs support, or send upstream security reports. The upstream repository's own contribution policy applies to upstream work and must not be used to route Abnormal product reports there.

Fork-specific issues and authorized pull requests belong only to `abnormalhq/stalwart`. Security-sensitive reports must follow this fork's private reporting guidance. Do not invent or publish contact addresses.

## Community and license scope

Do not enable the Enterprise feature in a Community build, bypass licensing checks, modify SEL-only source/snippets, or remove upstream copyright/license notices. Keep the explicit Community feature set and preserve protocol identifiers and storage compatibility.
